Evans Atula Osumba is a Kenyan full-stack and mobile software engineer based in Nairobi. He is the co-founder of Digital Leap Africa, a technology community focused on digital literacy and open-source collaboration across Africa. His work spans web platforms, agricultural technology, wildlife conservation software, and data analysis for public health research.

Osumba specializes in building scalable web and mobile solutions using Django, Laravel, React, and Flutter, with additional experience in data cleaning, statistical analysis, and developer mentorship.

Early life and education[edit]

Evans Atula Osumba is based in Nairobi, Kenya, where he studied software engineering at Edulink International College. He earned a Diploma in Information Systems Management (2023–2024) and an Advanced Diploma in Software Engineering (2024–2025), covering mobile development, enterprise applications, server management, and software testing.

Beyond formal education, Osumba has taught web development to underserved youth through the LEA Foundation in Mukuru Slums and has organized technical workshops and mentorship programs across Kenya as part of his STEM advocacy work.

Career[edit]

Tropical Institute of Community Health and Development in Africa

Since June 2026, Osumba has worked as a Junior Software Engineer (Intern) at Tropical Institute of Community Health and Development in Africa. He assists with developing and maintaining the organization's website and mobile applications.

  • Web development using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • Mobile app development using Flutter
  • Database management using MySQL

GEOHealth Hub — University of Nairobi

Since March 2026, Osumba has worked as a Junior Data Analyst (Intern) at GEOHealth Hub at the University of Nairobi. He assists with cleaning and analyzing health-related data from hospital records and school health assessments, as well as air quality data collected for research purposes.

  • Data cleaning and preprocessing of large health datasets
  • Statistical analysis for research publications
  • Data visualizations and stakeholder presentations

Animal IQ

From March 2025, Osumba has served as Innovation Lead at Animal IQ, a wildlife conservation technology initiative. He leads development of digital tools for conservation enthusiasts, researchers, and the general public across Kenya.

Digital Leap Africa

In January 2025, Osumba co-founded Digital Leap Africa as Community Lead. The organization has grown to over 600 members, hosted 20+ expert webinars, and mentored more than 100 aspiring developers.
